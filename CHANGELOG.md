# Changelog

## 1.2.4
- New app icon: check mark in a progress ring with a pencil, in the app's green, copper and cream. Also used for the site icon.
- Reading comfort in Settings: text size (normal / large / extra large) and a high-contrast switch.
- Simpler, more compact layout; cleaner More menu order.
- Grade 2 subjects and lesson lists now come only from the Ministry's Egyptian Baccalaureate site (Arabic, National History, English, Math, Physics, Chemistry, Programming and AI, Psychology, Financial Literacy; Business and Accounting list units only). Second foreign language has no published lessons there, so it is marked not available. The old grade 2 lists came from the previous system and were removed.

## 1.2.3
- Subjects follow the official Baccalaureate decision: grade 2 core subjects + one track elective (picked automatically, swap it from the syllabus tab), plus Financial Culture; grade 3 track subjects + Religion.
- Calmer syllabus screen: lesson actions hide behind a tap, filter (all / to do / done / weak), lessons grouped by unit, short subject chips with progress.
- Android back button now goes back one screen inside the app and only exits from the home screen.
- Softer spacing and shadows.

## 1.2.2
- Edit and reorder: rename lessons, change minutes of a study slot, edit to-dos, and move items up or down.
- To-do due date with an optional reminder notification.
- Built-in official lesson lists (Ministry of Education term 1 books, 2026/2027) for some subjects in grades 1 and 2. One tap loads them and skips lessons you already have.

## 1.2.1
- Past papers tracker: log scores per subject, see the average and trend.
- Weak topics: mark a lesson "ضعيف" and it gets more study time in the plan.
- Auto weekly plan: builds the week from exam dates, remaining lessons and your daily minutes, then adds it to your schedule in one tap.

## 1.2.0
- Home shows only "today"; other sections are collapsed.
- Works on tablet, laptop and desktop.
- Themes: classic, light, dark, sea, rose, auto.
- Revision reminders, mistakes log, backup, weekly report.
