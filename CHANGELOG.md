# Changelog

## 1.2.2
- Edit and reorder: rename lessons, change minutes of a study slot, edit to-dos, and move items up or down.
- To-do due date with an optional reminder notification.
- Built-in official lesson lists (Ministry of Education term 1 books, 2026/2027) for some subjects in grades 1 and 2. One tap loads them and skips lessons you already have.

## 1.2.1
- Past papers tracker: log scores per subject, see the average and trend.
- Weak topics: mark a lesson "ضعيف" and it gets more study time in the plan.
- Auto weekly plan: builds the week from exam dates, remaining lessons and your daily minutes, then adds it to your schedule in one tap.

## 1.2.0
- Home shows only "today"; other sections are collapsed.
- Works on tablet, laptop and desktop.
- Themes: classic, light, dark, sea, rose, auto.
- Revision reminders, mistakes log, backup, weekly report.
